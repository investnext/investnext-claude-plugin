How these are counted

Active investor
A contact InvestNext marks active, meaning they hold at least one live position. People are counted once, however many accounts they invest through.
Repeat investor
Someone who has contributed capital to two or more different deals, out of everyone who has contributed to at least one.
Funded, not signed
Money is recorded against a commitment whose paperwork isn’t complete.
Signed, no date
Paperwork is marked complete but has no completion date.
Amount mismatch
A commitment shows more recorded than committed, a $0 commitment has money against it, or an investor’s recorded commitments in a deal don’t equal their position’s contributions.
Wrong contact
The account on a commitment belongs to a different contact than the opportunity.
